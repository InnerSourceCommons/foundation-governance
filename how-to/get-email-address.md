# How to Get an ISC Email Address

All [members](https://innersourcecommons.org/about/members/) may receive an `@innersourcecommons.org` email address for times where they are representing the InnerSource Commons Foundation.
Reach out in the [#google-admins](https://innersourcecommons.slack.com/archives/C06V6RGLBP1) channel to ask for it.
