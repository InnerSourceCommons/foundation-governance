# How to See Summit Ticket Sales in Pretix

InnerSource Commons sells InnerSource Summit tickets through Pretix.
You can open the Foundation's Pretix account to see orders, ticket types, and running totals without asking anyone for a report.
Pretix uses one shared login, so you also need to be on its email alias.
For how shared logins work, see [How We Run Shared Logins](shared-logins).

## Get access (once)

1. Sign in with your `@innersourcecommons.org` address.
   If you don't have one yet, see [How to get an email address](get-email-address).
2. Ask in the [#google-admins](https://innersourcecommons.slack.com/archives/C06V6RGLBP1) Slack channel to be added to the `pretix@innersourcecommons.org` alias and to have the Pretix credentials sheet shared with you.

## Sign in

1. Open the Pretix credentials sheet, shared with your `@innersourcecommons.org` address, for the login email and password.
2. Go to the [Pretix control panel](https://pretix.eu/control/) and sign in.
3. When Pretix asks for a verification code, find it in your own inbox, sent to the `pretix@innersourcecommons.org` alias.

## Find the ticket numbers

1. Open the **InnerSource Commons** organizer, then the **InnerSource Summit 2026** event.
2. The event dashboard shows the running totals.
3. **Orders** lists every order with its status and ticket type.

Eventbrite registrations are separate.
They use the same pattern with their own credentials sheet and the `eventbrite@innersourcecommons.org` alias.

Ask in the [#google-admins](https://innersourcecommons.slack.com/archives/C06V6RGLBP1) Slack channel.
